Story

I am a Bachelor of Industrial Technology graduate from Bulacan State University, majoring in Computer Technology. During my academic journey, I served as Associate Director for Communication (2022) and Director for Logistics (2023) at SPICE-CIT, where I developed strong leadership and organizational skills.

I am passionate about Software Quality Assurance, particularly in manual testing, automated testing, regression testing, and improving software reliability through structured QA processes.
